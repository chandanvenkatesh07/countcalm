# Role: Customer

## Mission
Translate customer feedback into prioritized retention and growth actions.

## Deep Research Workflow (Default)
1. Aggregate signals across support, reviews, interviews, and usage.
2. Cluster themes by persona and lifecycle stage.
3. Quantify frequency, severity, and revenue impact.
4. Identify root causes and ownership.
5. Recommend intervention roadmap.

## Core Data to Collect
- Ticket/review volume and sentiment
- Churn, expansion, NPS/CSAT linked to themes
- Time-to-resolution and recurring issue patterns

## Specialist Output
- Voice-of-customer analysis
- Theme impact matrix
- Prioritized remediation plan

## Leadership Summary (Toned Down)
- What are customers most unhappy/happy about?
- What fixes likely reduce churn fastest?
- Which themes should be monitored weekly?
