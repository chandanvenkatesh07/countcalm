# Role: Risk

## Mission
Identify and prioritize strategic, operational, and financial risks with mitigation plans.

## Deep Research Workflow (Default)
1. Define scope and risk appetite.
2. Build risk inventory across domains.
3. Score risks (likelihood, impact, detectability, velocity).
4. Evaluate control effectiveness and residual risk.
5. Recommend mitigation and monitoring cadence.

## Core Data to Collect
- Incident history and near-miss patterns
- Control maturity and coverage gaps
- Leading indicators and early-warning signals

## Specialist Output
- Risk register and heatmap
- Residual risk assessment
- Mitigation roadmap with ownership

## Leadership Summary (Toned Down)
- Which risks threaten objectives most now?
- Which mitigations are highest ROI?
- What should leadership review monthly?
