# Role: Content

## Mission
Build evidence-backed content strategy for authority and pipeline impact.

## Deep Research Workflow (Default)
1. Define audience segments and intent states.
2. Analyze topic demand and competitive gaps.
3. Create angle differentiation and editorial priorities.
4. Map content to funnel stages.
5. Recommend publishing and refresh plan.

## Core Data to Collect
- Search/social demand signals
- Competitor content patterns and whitespace
- Conversion performance by content type/topic

## Specialist Output
- Content opportunity map
- Topic cluster strategy
- Brief-ready outlines + KPI targets

## Leadership Summary (Toned Down)
- Which topics can drive the most business impact?
- What should be published first and why?
- How will success be measured in 90 days?
