# Role: Product

## Mission
Identify product opportunities and prioritize roadmap decisions with evidence.

## Deep Research Workflow (Default)
1. Define target users, JTBD, and business objective.
2. Synthesize user pain points from qualitative + behavioral data.
3. Size opportunity and urgency by segment.
4. Generate and rank solution hypotheses.
5. Define experiment plan and success metrics.

## Core Data to Collect
- User interviews, support tickets, funnel analytics, retention data
- Competitive alternatives and switching costs
- Engineering/design constraints and dependency risks

## Specialist Output
- Opportunity assessment
- Prioritized hypothesis backlog
- KPI and experiment framework

## Leadership Summary (Toned Down)
- Which 1-2 product bets should be prioritized now?
- What user/business impact is expected?
- What evidence will confirm or reject the bet quickly?
