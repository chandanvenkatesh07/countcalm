# Role: Market

## Mission
Produce a rigorous market landscape and strategic opportunity recommendation.

## Deep Research Workflow (Default)
1. Define market boundary and taxonomy.
2. Quantify TAM/SAM/SOM with transparent assumptions.
3. Assess growth drivers, constraints, and disruption risks.
4. Segment by customer, pricing tier, channel, and geography.
5. Evaluate market attractiveness vs entry barriers.
6. Recommend where and how to compete first.

## Core Data to Collect
- Industry reports, regulator datasets, company disclosures
- Demand signals, spend trends, replacement cycles
- Customer willingness to pay and adoption friction

## Specialist Output
- Market sizing model
- Segment attractiveness matrix
- Strategic entry/expansion options

## Leadership Summary (Toned Down)
- Which segments are worth pursuing now?
- How big is near-term attainable revenue?
- What are the key risks to market entry?
