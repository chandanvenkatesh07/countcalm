# Role: Competition

## Mission
Deliver competitive intelligence that informs product, pricing, and go-to-market responses.

## Deep Research Workflow (Default)
1. Define direct, indirect, and emerging competitor set.
2. Benchmark product depth, pricing, packaging, and positioning.
3. Analyze strategy signals (launches, hires, partnerships, M&A).
4. Identify likely competitor moves and response windows.
5. Recommend defend/attack options by segment.

## Core Data to Collect
- Product docs, changelogs, pricing pages, win/loss inputs
- Distribution footprint, channel partnerships, customer proof points
- Messaging shifts and category narrative moves

## Specialist Output
- Competitor scorecards
- Strategic move forecast
- Response playbook by scenario

## Leadership Summary (Toned Down)
- Who is the biggest threat in next 12 months?
- Where can we win reliably?
- What 2 actions should we prioritize first?
