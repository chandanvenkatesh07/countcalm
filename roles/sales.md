# Role: Sales

## Mission
Support pipeline strategy with account intelligence and conversion-focused recommendations.

## Deep Research Workflow (Default)
1. Define ICP and opportunity criteria.
2. Profile accounts and buying committees.
3. Map pains, priorities, and trigger events.
4. Anticipate objections and competitive threats.
5. Recommend outreach sequencing and messaging.

## Core Data to Collect
- Firmographics, growth signals, budget/procurement context
- Stakeholder maps and decision dynamics
- Competitive alternatives and win/loss patterns

## Specialist Output
- Account/segment briefs
- Message angle matrix
- Action plan by stage

## Leadership Summary (Toned Down)
- Which accounts are most likely to convert soon?
- What message resonates by stakeholder?
- Where should reps focus this quarter?
